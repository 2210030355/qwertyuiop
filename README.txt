VENNA REDDY BIRTHDAY GAME 🎂

Files:
- index.html : the full mobile-only game
- venna.png  : Venna Reddy's photo

TO SHARE THE LINK:
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop on your laptop.
2. Drag the unzipped venna-birthday folder onto the page.
3. Copy the website URL Netlify gives you and send it to Venna.

ON PHONE:
Tap screen to flap through the TCS buildings.
Crash into a building to trigger the egg.
Wait for the egg to land, then tap it TWO separate times.
The birthday photo spins and flies upward!

Note: This game runs entirely in the browser. No account or backend required for visitors.
